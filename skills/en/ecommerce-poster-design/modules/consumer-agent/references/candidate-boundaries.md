# Adjacent-Level Boundaries and Candidate References (1.5.3)

Read this with `subcriteria-anchors.md`. These are level-selection conditions, not human gold labels for the 81 C-library cases. Determine requirements and critical purchase concerns from the complete task before viewing the poster; never use prior scores or choose easy concerns after viewing. First record location, visible fact, and impact, then compare adjacent levels. Level 4 requires the item's explicit enhancement condition, not attractive style alone. For factual items, confirmed correct remains 4 and confirmed wrong remains 0.

Candidate cases are expression references only. Never transfer their prices, performance, official identity, or product structure to the current task, and never claim to have reinspected a case when its image is unavailable. Distinguish unreadable from absent and missing input from confirmed error. Provenance awaiting A/C confirmation is not itself missing fact: score against the supplied consistent baseline while noting that scoring is not independent fact certification or production-handoff approval.

Decide from the task whether price, CTA, or a specific scene is required. Apply existing D3 N/A rules strictly. P-M-S tags do not change weights or prove fit. A scene may be expressed through copy, action, structure, or environment; a person/background is not mandatory. If tag and brief conflict, ask A/C to reconcile rather than reclassify. Assign each root defect to one primary item and record secondary impact without duplicate deduction: D2 asks whether benefit is explained, D4 whether the locked need/context fits, and D5 whether evidence, concerns, and action reasons are credible.

The operational 2→3 and 3→4 boundaries are the explicit transitions in `subcriteria-anchors.md`: level 3 requires the stated relation to work clearly; level 4 additionally requires the stated enhanced clarity, priority, completeness, directness, or mutually supporting relationship. Factual subcriteria D3_1 and D5_1 do not use a gradual experiential scale. Keep transaction price/offer nature, value, unit, qualifiers, conditions, and CTA roles distinct; never calculate a combined price that the input did not state.

Freeze the same-task D5 concern list before viewing, using only brief, confirmed claims, and locked M/S, in task order. Record each as `concern — input-supported answer — observable expression`. Reuse the list throughout the same test track and do not change it from current imagery or scores. D5_4 level 2 has important unanswered or generic concerns; level 3 gives a specific input-supported answer for every concern; level 4 makes every answer and necessary condition clear with no critical comprehension gap. D5_2 tests factual support and comprehensibility of those answers; do not double-deduct the same defect. Do not demand a certification, test, or condition not required and not provided merely to reach level 4.

Keep formal output in A-D-2.0 and the 25-item draft schema. Evidence records location, visible fact, and satisfied boundary, with a case ID only when genuinely used. Two reviewers calibrate independently against the complete brief and facts, save image version/display size/disputed conditions, and treat formal human labels as stronger than candidate descriptions. Repeated scoring with fixed task/image/model/rubric plus single-variable degradation/repair and held-out cases is required to test stability; script regression proves only contract/calculation integrity, not visual reliability or recommendation quality.
