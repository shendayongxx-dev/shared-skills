# P-M-S Evaluation Lenses

P population, M purchase motivation, and S usage scene come from the upstream C library. Apply the locked tags; never reclassify them.

P01 young students/trend newcomers: novelty, appearance, acceptable price, social expression. P02 professionals/commuters: efficiency, convenience, quality, professional presentation, reliability. P03 families/parents: safety, health, durability, ease, family use. P04 mature quality seekers: material, craft, brand trust, experience, long-term value. P05 practical mass consumers: price, utility, durability, clear offers. P06 older health-conscious consumers: safety, health, comfort, trust, readability, ease of use. P primarily affects population/scene fit and the readability threshold across information.

M01 price benefit requires evidence of price, offer, quantity, entitlement, period, or conditions. M02 functional efficiency requires function, result, time/effort saving, or convenience. M03 quality trust requires material, craft, certification, brand, durability, or warranty. M04 professional performance requires professional task, comparable parameter, test, or certification. M05 aesthetic self-expression requires form, color, style, individuality, or pleasure. M06 emotional gifting requires recipient, festival relationship, gift packaging, or emotional expression. M primarily affects benefit communication and purchase confidence; accept only input-supported evidence.

S01 home life checks actual household place/action/convenience/comfort. S02 work/study checks product relation to office, meeting, campus, or task. S03 commute/vehicle checks mobility, carrying, transit, or in-car use. S04 outdoor/sport/travel checks real use in exercise, camping, hiking, or travel. S05 leisure/social checks sharing, interaction, relaxed social setting, and social product presence. S06 festival/gifting checks recipient, formal gifting context, and gift value. Atmosphere alone is insufficient; the product must have a real use relation.

If the image appears to target another population or scene, report insufficient/conflicting expression of the locked tag; do not rewrite it. Diagnose scene atmosphere and product benefit separately when they are distinct, but never double-deduct one defect. Consumer scoring includes visual character only when it affects recognition, understanding, trust, or action; leave pure visual craft to the Aesthetic Agent.
